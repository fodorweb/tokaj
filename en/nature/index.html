Discover the character of Tokaj

Tokaj-Hegyalja is best explored with curiosity and time. Its wines, villages, vineyards and cultural heritage are closely connected, and every visit can reveal a different perspective.

Ideas for your visit

  • Visit a winery and book a guided tasting.
  • Explore a historic village or cellar row.
  • Walk or cycle through the vineyard landscape.
  • Pair local food with wines from the region.

Good to know

Opening hours, tasting availability and seasonal services vary. Confirm details directly before travelling.
