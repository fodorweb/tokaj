A fragrant variety for expressive dry and sweet wines.

Why it matters in Tokaj

The region’s volcanic soils, varied slopes and long growing season give local grape varieties room to express different styles. The same grape can produce fresh, mineral dry wines or richly layered sweet wines.

What to look for

Compare wines from different villages, vineyard sites and producers. A side-by-side tasting is one of the best ways to understand the diversity of Tokaj.
