Where to stay

A guide to boutique hotels, guesthouses and rural stays.

Choose your base according to the kind of trip you want: a walkable town stay, a village guesthouse or a countryside retreat.

Tokaj town

Good for riverside walks, restaurants and a first-time visit without moving the car constantly.

Mád & nearby villages

Ideal for a wine-focused stay close to vineyards and small cellar experiences.

Rural guesthouses

A slower pace, gardens and village atmosphere. Confirm transport options before booking.

What to check

Parking, breakfast, late check-in, cancellation terms and distance to tastings.

Availability and rates change frequently; check directly with each property.
