Tokaj town

Where the Bodrog and Tisza meet: historic cellars and riverside life.

Where the Bodrog and Tisza meet: historic cellars and riverside life. The best way to understand this place is to slow down: walk the streets, look towards the hills and leave time for a local cellar or meal.

What to experience

  • Explore the historic centre and local architecture.
  • Look for cellar rows and vineyard viewpoints nearby.
  • Plan a tasting with a local producer and book ahead.
  • Combine this stop with a nearby village for a full day.

Make it part of a route

Opening hours, cellar visits and local services vary by season. Confirm details directly before travelling.
