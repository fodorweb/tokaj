Borkóstolási útmutató

Hogyan kóstolj tokaji borokat és tervezz pincelátogatást.

Tokaji wines reward attention. Start with the aroma, notice the balance between acidity and sweetness, and take time with the finish.

A simple tasting sequence

  1. Look at the colour and clarity.
  2. Swirl gently and identify fruit, floral, spice and mineral notes.
  3. Taste and notice acidity, sweetness, texture and length.
  4. Try the wine with food; pairing can reveal new details.

Serving tips

Serve dry whites cool but not ice-cold. Sweet wines are often best slightly chilled in smaller glasses. Follow producer guidance and enjoy responsibly.
