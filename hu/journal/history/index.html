A tokaji bor története

Bevezetés a tokaji borkultúra évszázados történetébe.

Tokaj’s wine culture developed over centuries, shaped by geography, trade routes, vineyard communities and the international reputation of its sweet wines.

A wine with a long reach

Tokaji wines were prized far beyond Hungary and became part of the region’s cultural identity. Historic cellars, vineyard names and local traditions still connect contemporary winemakers with this past.

Tradition and renewal

Today, producers work across classic sweet wines, dry single-vineyard Furmint and modern interpretations.
