TOKAJ-HEGYALJA · HUNGARY

Vulkáni talajok és dűlők

A geológia, kitettség és dűlő szerepe a borok karakterében.

Tokaj-Hegyalja is a varied landscape of volcanic hills, valleys and river influence. Geology, slope, aspect and local climate contribute to vineyard character.

Volcanic foundations

Rhyolite, tuff, andesite and other volcanic materials occur across the region, alongside loess and clay-rich soils.

Why vineyard sites matter

Comparing wines from different dűlő (vineyard sites) is a rewarding way to explore Tokaj. Ask producers about exposure, soil and the story behind each bottling.
