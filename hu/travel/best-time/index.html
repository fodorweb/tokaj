Mikor érdemes érkezni?

Évszakos élmények a tavaszi dűlőtúráktól a szüretig.

Tokaj is rewarding in every season, but the experience changes with the vineyard calendar.

Spring · March–May

Fresh green hills, quieter cellar visits and comfortable walking weather.

Summer · June–August

Long days, outdoor dining, festivals and active trips. Book accommodation early.

Autumn · September–November

Harvest atmosphere, colourful vineyards and a busy time for wineries. Book tastings ahead.

Winter · December–February

A quieter visit focused on cellar hospitality and food. Check seasonal opening schedules.
