Útiterv-javaslatok

Ötletek hétvégére, boros napra vagy hosszabb kiruccanáshoz.

Use these sample routes as a starting point. Cellar visits and village walks are best enjoyed without rushing.

One day: first taste of Tokaj

  1. Morning: Arrive in Tokaj, walk the riverside and explore the historic centre.
  2. Lunch: Choose a local restaurant and try regional dishes.
  3. Afternoon: Pre-book a winery visit and compare a dry Furmint with a sweet wine.
  4. Evening: Stay for sunset or continue to your accommodation.

Two days: villages and vineyards

Day one: Tokaj town, winery visit and relaxed dinner. Day two: Mád and Tarcal, a vineyard viewpoint and a cellar tasting.

Three days: culture and nature

Add Sárospatak’s heritage, the cellar rows of Hercegkút and a walk or cycle route in the wider landscape.
