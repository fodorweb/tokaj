TOKAJ-HEGYALJA · HUNGARY

Éttermek és helyi ízek

Helyi ízek, hagyományos ételek és borpárosítások.

Tokaj is a wine destination, but the table is part of the story. Look for seasonal ingredients, freshwater fish, local cheeses and traditional Hungarian dishes.

What to try

  • Freshwater fish dishes from the Tisza and Bodrog region.
  • Regional soups, stews and seasonal vegetables.
  • Local cheeses and charcuterie with dry Furmint.
  • Fruit desserts with late-harvest or Aszú wines.

Pairing ideas

Dry Furmint works with fish and hard cheeses. Aromatic Hárslevelű complements herbs and spice. Sweet Tokaji wines pair with blue cheese and fruit desserts.
