TOKAJ-HEGYALJA · HUNGARY

Megközelítés és közlekedés

Utazás autóval, vonattal és busszal, helyi közlekedési tippek.

Tokaj-Hegyalja lies in north-eastern Hungary. The region can be reached by car or public transport, but moving between smaller villages is easier with a planned route.

By car

Driving offers flexibility for visiting several villages and vineyard viewpoints. Arrange a designated driver if you plan wine tastings.

By train or bus

Check current timetables and connections before departure; local services may be less frequent on weekends.

Getting around

Consider taxis, pre-arranged transfers, cycling routes or staying within walking distance of evening plans.
