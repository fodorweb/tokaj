TOKAJ-HEGYALJA · HUNGARY

Száraz tokaji borok

Friss, feszes fehérborok vulkanikus talajról és helyi fajtákból.

Dry Tokaji wines show another side of the region: energetic whites with freshness, texture and a strong sense of place.

Furmint at the centre

Furmint’s naturally high acidity and ability to express vineyard differences make it especially suited to dry wines. Hárslevelű adds aromatic range and texture.

Food pairing

Try dry Furmint with freshwater fish, roast chicken, grilled vegetables and hard cheeses.
