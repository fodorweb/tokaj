Tokaji aszú

A legendás botritiszes édes bor készítése, felszolgálása és élvezete.

Tokaji Aszú is one of the world’s great naturally sweet wines, made from individually selected botrytised berries.

How Aszú is made

In favourable autumn conditions, Botrytis cinerea (noble rot) concentrates the berries and transforms their aromas. The shrivelled Aszú berries are collected and processed with care, then fermented and matured.

What does it taste like?

Expect layers of dried apricot, citrus peel, honey, tea, spice and a long finish. The exact profile depends on vintage, vineyard and producer.

Serving and pairing

Serve lightly chilled. Try it with blue cheese, fruit desserts or simply on its own.
