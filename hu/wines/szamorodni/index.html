Szamorodni

Egyedi tokaji stílus egészséges és töppedt szemeket is tartalmazó fürtökből.

Szamorodni is made from bunches harvested as they come, with a mixture of healthy and botrytised berries.

Dry and sweet expressions

Szamorodni can be made in dry or sweet styles. Dry examples may develop oxidative, nutty notes through maturation, while sweet examples offer richness balanced by acidity.

How to enjoy it

Try with roast poultry, mushrooms, aged cheese or desserts depending on the style.
