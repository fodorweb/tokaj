Tokaji borlexikon

Puttony, botritisz, aszú, dűlő és további szakkifejezések.

Aszú

Botrytised berries selected individually for the region’s iconic sweet wine.

Botrytis

Noble rot, a fungus that can concentrate grapes under suitable conditions.

Dűlő

A named vineyard site or parcel.

Furmint

The leading grape variety of Tokaj.

Gönci hordó

A traditional Hungarian barrel size associated with Tokaj.

Hárslevelű

An aromatic local grape variety.

Puttony

A traditional measure historically used to describe Aszú richness.

Szamorodni

A wine made from whole bunches with healthy and botrytised berries.
